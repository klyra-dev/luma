# Code of Conduct

Last updated: 2026-04-09

## Our Pledge
In the interest of fostering an open, welcoming, and productive community, contributors and maintainers of the `luma_control` project agree to abide by this Code of Conduct. We value respectful, constructive interactions and aim to make this project a safe place for people of all backgrounds and identities.

## Expected Behavior
All participants are expected to:
- Be considerate, respectful, and professional in communication.
- Be welcoming to newcomers and provide constructive feedback.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully; assume good intent.
- Focus on the technical issue at hand, not on personal attributes.

## Unacceptable Behavior
Unacceptable behavior includes, but is not limited to:
- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind.
- Abusive, derogatory, or demeaning language or actions.
- Personal attacks, public shaming, or trolling.
- Derailing discussions, doxxing, or sharing private information.
- Spamming or other disruptive actions that impede collaboration.

## Reporting
If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the project maintainers by opening an issue or sending email to the contact below. When reporting, include:
- A short summary of the incident.
- Relevant dates, times, and links (issues, pull requests, comments).
- Any steps already taken to address the situation.

Reports will be handled with discretion. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith will not be tolerated.

## Enforcement
Project maintainers are responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct. Consequences for unacceptable behavior may include:
- A private warning
- Removal of offending content
- Temporary or permanent suspension of contributor privileges
- Banning from project spaces (issues, PRs, chat, etc.)

Decisions about enforcement will be made by maintainers after reviewing the facts and may involve consultation with other trusted community members. The goal of enforcement is to remedy harmful behavior and preserve a healthy community.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces including:
- Issue trackers and pull requests
- Project mailing lists and chat rooms
- Events and meetups organized by the project
- Any other place where community interaction related to this project occurs
